Deliverables

What's a share unit?

One original post pointing your audience to your petition.

The partnership requires 3 posts at minimum — 1 primary video or feed post on your highest-engagement platform, plus 2 secondary shares in any format (story, tweet, threads, newsletter).

3 is the floor — not the ceiling. The program has no cap on posts. The more you share, the more signatures you drive — and the more you earn each month.
Each share must be unique creative. Secondary shares must be posted on a platform with 50K+ followers to qualify. Every post must include #ad or #sponsored.

Your voice, all the way through
