Payment FAQ

The boring (important) stuff.

Money questions, answered straight. No fine print.

When do I get the advance?
48 hours after you sign. Direct deposit via Gigapay. We've processed 342 payouts — average actual time is 31 hours.
When does revenue share pay out?
~120 days after petition launch. The petition accrues revenue for 90 days, then we close the window and pay out 30 days later. One single deposit.
What about taxes?
You're an independent contractor. We issue a 1099 in January for any year you earned $600+ from us. Set aside about 25-30% for taxes — we don't withhold.
If the petition flops, do I still get paid?
Yes — the advance is yours regardless. The advance is for the work (creating + sharing). The revenue share is for the result. You always get the first; the second is upside.
Is there a clawback if the petition gets pulled?
Only if you violate the content terms. Hate speech, fraud, posts that violate platform policy. Standard stuff. If we ever ask for the advance back, the contract spells out exactly why.
