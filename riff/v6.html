04 — Share units

What's a share unit?

One original post pointing to your petition
3 is the floor — not the ceiling.

The partnership requires 3 posts — 1 primary on your highest-engagement platform, plus 2 secondary shares in any format (story, tweet, threads, newsletter).

The program has no cap on posts. The more you share, the more signatures you drive — and the more you earn each month.

Each share must be unique creative. Secondaries must be on a platform with 50K+ followers to qualify. Every post must include #ad or #sponsored.
