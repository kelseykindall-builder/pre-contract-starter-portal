The problem with brand deals

Most sponsorships ask you to be slightly less yourself.

A script you didn't write. A product you wouldn't use. A caption your manager negotiated. The brand pays for your reach — and quietly muffles the thing that built it. Your audience can tell. Engagement drops. The next deal pays a little less.

This is the opposite of that.

We're not asking you to read our copy. We're asking you to write yours — about a cause you already speak to, in a voice your audience already trusts. A research study at Stanford last year showed creators under low-restriction contracts generated 53 clicks per post, versus 14 under standard ones. The thing that converts is the thing you'd post anyway.

We pay for that. Up front. With upside if it scales.

What we believe

Five things we hold to before we ever talk money.

N° 01

Your voice is the asset. Not your reach.

We don't optimize for follower count. We optimize for whether your audience actually listens when you speak on a topic. Trust is the unit, not impressions.

N° 02

You pick the cause.

If we wanted you to argue our position, we'd hire a copywriter. We want you to argue your own — about something your audience already trusts you on.

N° 03

Money should be honest, not hidden.

Guaranteed advance up front. Promo revenue share on top. No "we'll send you free product." No barter. Real money, predictable structure, on the contract.

N° 04

Selective is a feature.

We turn down most creators we evaluate. The roster stays small on purpose. When you're in, you're in because your voice carries — not because we hit a quota.

N° 05

The work outlives the post.

Brand deals expire. Petitions don't. Three years from now your petition could still be cited in a legislative brief. That outcome is what we mean by "this matters."

N° 06

If it doesn't feel right, don't sign.

Book a call with Kelsey before you commit to anything. We'd rather walk away than ship a partnership that doesn't fit. Your audience will be there next year.

For the practical question you're going to ask anyway

The partnership, plainly stated.

What you do.
  • Sign the contract (DocuSign, ~5 minutes).
  • Pick a cause and draft a petition. We help if you'd like; we don't override your voice.
  • Share it three times across your platforms, in your style, on your schedule.
  • Stay reachable for ~8 weeks for promo questions.
What we do.
  • Pay you a guaranteed advance within 48 hours of signing. The amount is on your contract — locked, tier-based, never guessed at.
  • Pay a revenue share for every signer your audience drives, accrued over 90 days.
  • Provide a real human (Kelsey) to answer any question, fast.
  • Tell the truth, including when the answer is uncomfortable.
