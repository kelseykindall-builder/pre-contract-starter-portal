What's a share unit?

One original post pointing your audience to your petition.

The partnership requires 3 posts — 1 primary on your highest-engagement platform, plus 2 secondary shares in any format you like.

3 is the floor — not the ceiling. You need 3 to keep your advance, but the program has no cap on posts. The more you share, the more you earn.
