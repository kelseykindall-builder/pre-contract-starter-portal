Deliverables

What's a share unit?

One original post pointing your audience to your petition.

The partnership requires 3 posts — 1 primary on your highest-engagement platform, plus 2 secondary shares in any format (story, tweet, threads, newsletter).

3 is the floor — not the ceiling. The program has no cap on posts. The more you share, the more signatures you drive — and the more you earn each month.
Each share must be unique creative. Secondary shares must be on a platform with 50K+ followers to qualify. Every post must include #ad or #sponsored.

Why it's different

Why this isn't a typical brand deal

Built for creators, not commodified by them

Your voice, all the way through

You pick the topic. You write the petition. You post in your style. Our team helps shape it for maximum impact — but the message is ultimately yours.

Guaranteed payment, no signature target

We activate your advance when you share your first post. You keep it whether your petition gets 1,000 or 100,000 signatures. Performance is an opportunity, not a gate.

No earnings ceiling

Once your petition recoups your advance, you earn 95% of promotions revenue — every month, for as long as it keeps gaining signatures.

The chance to keep going

Once your petition's revenue surpasses your advance, you unlock the option to start another petition* — fresh earnings stacking on top of what's already running.

*Not guaranteed — offered to ambassadors who've shown they can mobilize their audience.
